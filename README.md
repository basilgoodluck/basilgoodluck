I'm Basil, and I build software.

My experience spans across AI systems, web automation, trading infrastructure, and backend systems in general. I use **[PostgreSQL](https://postgresql.org)** a lot; at this point, it’s just second nature. For deployment, I stick with **[Docker](https://docker.com)** and run everything on **[Ubuntu](https://ubuntu.com)**.

Lately, I’ve been deep into trading strategy design and AI startups, trying to turn ideas into something that actually holds up in real use.

I’ve built things like [AIPE](https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1431460121904418919), a sniper bot that lives inside Discord, and [Quasar](https://quasar-ai-agent.vercel.app), an AI agent I worked on during a Lablab AI hackathon. Alongside that, I run [NobleQuant](https://www.noblequant.pro/), where I build algorithmic trading systems, from strategy design and implementation to risk management and fixing what breaks when systems meet real markets.

I’m open to working on web and mobile software projects.
